# hand_bridge --backend dm: the DM arm's gripper behind the Hand protocol (A2)

Operator 2026-10-09 (project `docs/status/2026-10-09-remaining-migrations-design.md`, A2): the DM gripper (motor 7 on the DM
arm's CAN bus, a DM4310) is served by `hand_bridge` on the RT box, over the same line protocol as the Franka Hand. Decided:
MOVE = on-motor MIT PD at the grasp gate's 40 / 2, with the target ramped at the commanded speed. A 3 N.m target clamp
(kp * error <= 3). The gripper is independent of the arm's ARM. GRASP is refused until the rung-11 N->torque map exists.
Bridge loss: exit-time disable now; the motor's CAN-timeout register is decided at the bench.

* `rt/src/hand_device.hpp` (new): the device seam the bridge serves (`HandDevice`: read / move / homing / grasp / thread-safe
  stop; `DeviceLost` = reconnect, `ActionEnded` = the session is fine). `HandSample` replaces `franka::GripperState` in
  `observe_state`.
* `rt/src/hand_bridge.cpp`: the actor, the stop worker, admission and the wire are unchanged and now run over `HandDevice`.
  The Franka Hand is `FrankaHand` (libfranka's exceptions mapped onto the seam, as the actor classified them before:
  NetworkException = lost, other franka::Exception = action ended), under `HAND_WITH_FRANKA`. The bridge's envelope
  (`bridge_limits`) comes from its backend. A GRASP on a gripper without force grasp is REJECTed `uncalibrated_force`.
  `--backend dm` flags (every value required, nothing assumed). SIGTERM/SIGINT go to one thread that releases the device
  (DM: zero torque, then disable) and exits. The positional form (`robot_ip [port [bind]]`, the deployed unit) is
  unchanged.
* `rt/src/hand_dm.hpp` (new): `DmGripper` + `SocketCanPort` (one socket, filtered to the motor's Master ID, CAN FD frames as
  the arm backend sends them; replies routed by the payload nibble, foreign ones ignored). It runs a 100 Hz servo thread:
  - before the first action: zero-gain frames only (a reply, no authority) and no enable;
  - an action: enable once, then ramp and clamp;
  - success: the measured width settles within 1 mm of the goal;
  - blocked short of the goal: fail and hold the measured angle;
  - no settle by the travel time + 2 s: fail and hold where it is;
  - GSTOP: hold measured;
  - silence (50 ms) or shutdown: zero torque, then disable.
* `rt/src/dm_mit.hpp` (new): the MIT codec moved out of `backend_dm.cpp` (no behaviour change; `dm_backend_selfcheck`,
  `dm_command_selfcheck`, `torque_cap_selfcheck` pass).
* `rt/CMakeLists.txt`: `hand_bridge` is always built (DM), linked to libfranka only `WITH_FRANKA`.
  `hand_bridge_selfcheck` no longer needs libfranka. New `hand_dm_selfcheck`. Both are registered with CTest.
* `end_effectors/sim_hand_bridge.py`: the same `uncalibrated_force` reason.
* `rt/README.md`: the DM backend.

Constants this introduces (the bridge's own; for OPERATOR CONFIRMATION, not decided by the operator):
* servo frame period 10 ms (100 Hz);
* settle window 0.2 s / 0.5 mm and success 1 mm (the sim Hand's values);
* an unsettled move fails after its travel time + 2 s;
* silence bound 50 ms (the arm backend's per-motor deadman);
* the measured width is clamped to the stroke within 2 mm (the Franka bridge's open-end allowance). Beyond it no STATE is
  sent.

Named, not changed:
* The kd term (kd * dq) is outside the 3 N.m bound: the clamp bounds kp * error only, as decided.
* The MIT codes truncate: kp 40 is sent as 39.93; the zero-torque frame is -0.002 N.m.
* A crashed bridge leaves the motor on its last frame (bench decision).
* No DM unit/registry instance: the deployed `--speed-max` (no existing figure: today's open is an unramped step), the CAN
  interface and the RT box placement are the operator's.
* `HandGraspFsm`/`HandPanel` still resolve configured defaults against the Franka Hand at construction, and the panel
  shows Close enabled on a no-force gripper (a click is refused). Both belong to the DM graphs' slice.
* Deploy order: PC clients before a DM bridge (an old client refuses widths above 0.08 m).

Checks:
* `hand_dm_selfcheck`, against a fake DM4310 (quasi-static finger, an object, silence):
  - the map and config bounds;
  - no session without replies, and no enable or authority before the first action;
  - MOVE no faster than commanded, settling within 1 mm, every authority frame within kp * |q_des - q| <= 3 N.m (+1 code);
  - a blocked move fails and holds measured;
  - GSTOP mid-move returns within 200 ms and holds;
  - GRASP refused;
  - silence and shutdown both release (zero torque, then disable, exactly once);
  - foreign replies ignored.
  - Mutants killed: no clamp, no hold on a block, stop without ending the ramp.
* `hand_bridge_selfcheck`: with the no-force envelope, LIMITS on the wire, `uncalibrated_force`, and a wider MOVE admitted.
  Built offline and with libfranka 0.21.2.
* `check_sim_hand`; the CLI's refusals and SIGTERM release run offline (no CAN device).

Nothing ran on hardware or a CAN bus.
